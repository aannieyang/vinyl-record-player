CURRENT UF2 FILES: ADC MASTER (2026-11-19)
BCK physical pin 21 / GP16 -> ADC BCK + DAC BCK (ADC drives)
LRCK physical pin 22 / GP17 -> ADC LRCK + DAC LCK (ADC drives)
ADC DOUT physical pin 24 / GP18
DAC DIN physical pin 25 / GP19
passthrough-status.uf2: ADC SCKI physical pin 27 / GP21 (12 MHz from Pico)
tone-status.uf2 and silence-status.uf2: ADC SCKI physical pin 17 / GP13
The passthrough image now contains simple mono passthrough and voltage prints.
The tone/silence images are older diagnostic builds, not generated by the
current simplified source. Their MCLK is still GP13, physical pin 17.
ADC MD1=MD0=HIGH, FMT=LOW. fs=46875 Hz.
All three images require ADC-generated clocks, even tone/silence.
See ../DEBUGGING.txt for details. Legacy images use incompatible wiring and
clock directions; do not flash them into this ADC-master setup.
